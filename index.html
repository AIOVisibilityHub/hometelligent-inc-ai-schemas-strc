About Hometelligent Inc.

Hometelligent Inc. is a Bay Area residential design-build and general contracting company based in Albany, California. The company serves homeowners throughout Alameda, Contra Costa, Marin, and San Francisco counties, delivering planning, permitting coordination, construction management, remodeling, additions, and specialty residential improvements.

Published resources cover ADU construction, Detached ADU construction, Attached ADU construction, Backyard cottage construction, Garage conversion ADUs, Garage-to-living-space conversions, Basement ADU conversions, In-law suite construction, Guest house construction, Granny flat construction, and 355 additional service areas.

Service coverage includes Alameda, Albany, Berkeley, Dublin, Emeryville, Fremont, Hayward, Livermore, Newark, Oakland and 395 more communities.
